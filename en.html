DIJITAL YOKLAMA — PRIVACY NOTICE (KVKK)

Version: 2026-11-05.1

Controller and contact

Halil Arda Çulha operates platform accounts and this service. Contact: halilardailetisim@gmail.com. This notice covers registered and guest users, participants without accounts, and parents/legal guardians. Organizers determine their own event/roster purposes and must establish their own lawful basis and information process.

Data and purposes

Firebase Authentication processes your sign-in identity, provider and email where applicable. Passwords are not stored in Firestore or QR codes. Guests also have central accounts. Firestore may hold names, usernames, random app identifiers, event details, staff/participant links, attendance status/times, audit records, questions and form answers. The service uses these to operate accounts, events, attendance, access controls and corrections. Event creators can read form answers; authorized event staff can read rosters and attendance. Ordinary participants cannot read other participants’ lists or answers.

Collection and legal basis

Data is obtained electronically from users, organizer uploads, QR and app actions. Necessary requested account/service processing is assessed under KVKK Article 5(2)(c), and proportionate security/integrity processing under Article 5(2)(f). Applicable legal obligations and establishment/exercise/protection of rights have their own Article 5(2) bases. Optional photo sharing has a separate, withdrawable explicit-consent flow. A general checkbox does not authorize every roster or form purpose. Avoid health details, national IDs and unnecessary personal information; special-category collection is not the intended use.

Children

The app does not request birth dates, age bands or identity documents and does not classify users as children or adults. Photo sharing is off by default and optional for all accounts. This notice is not age verification or parental consent. The publisher or organizer must provide an appropriate notice and lawful basis, and handle a legal representative process where required, before processing children's information. Acknowledging this policy is not a substitute for those requirements.

Optional photos Photo sharing is off by default for all accounts. If separately enabled, other signed-in app accounts may see your small chosen photo or Google profile image when looking up identities or displaying participants. Displaying a Google URL requests its image server. Disabling removes the app copy/link, not your original Google account photo. External downloaded/copied files require separate handling. Sharing a photo is not required for attendance.

Recipients and international processing

The service uses Firebase/Google. Firebase Authentication operates in the US; Firestore data can be processed abroad in the publisher-selected region and under the provider’s conditions. Google and relevant subprocessors, authorized organizers/staff and lawfully competent authorities may be recipients within their roles. The publisher must establish a valid KVKK Article 9 mechanism for ongoing international transfers before public release. Reading this notice or checking a general consent box is not a substitute for that mechanism or necessary contractual/notification steps.

Device and notifications

QR reading and printed-name recognition on signature sheets run on-device. The app does not upload signature-sheet photos or automatically verify signatures. Operating-system file/photo providers have their own services. Pending attendance and the inbox use encrypted local storage. The inbox holds up to 100 entries for 90 days and is pruned when the app runs. Local reminders are scheduled on-device and lock-screen messages omit names, QR content and form answers. No FCM device tokens are collected in this version; newly arriving invitations are not remotely pushed while the app is closed. Camera/notification system permissions are not KVKK consent. Advertising/analytics tracking and automatic crash-report sharing are not enabled.

Retention and deletion

Account/event data must be limited to the necessary service period and applicable obligations, not a universal statutory term. In-app account deletion resumes after interruption and removes associated accessible records. Organizer-uploaded names in other accounts’ events and externally exported files are not automatically erased everywhere; use the contact channel for these. Organizers must define and implement their own retention/deletion needs. Spark does not run a scheduled server cleanup while the app is closed; the operator must perform retention and request reviews separately.

Rights and requests

Contact halilardailetisim@gmail.com for KVKK Article 11 access, information, purpose/recipient details, correction, applicable erasure/destruction and recipient notification, objections to exclusively automated adverse analysis and damages requests. Participants without an account and representatives may also apply. Identity/authority checks must be proportionate; do not send passwords, national ID numbers or identity documents with the first message. Requests are assessed within 30 days. The in-app profile/privacy file is a partial export; use this channel for a complete request.

Consent and updates

This notice and explicit consent are separate. Continuing records that the notice has been presented, not blanket consent to all processing. Optional photo permission can be withdrawn in Settings → Privacy. Refusing optional photo sharing does not prevent basic service. Material new purposes trigger an updated notice and renewed information. The original Turkish notice is also available.
